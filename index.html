Independent data assessment

I find out whether an organization's data can prove what it actually did.

Ravikanth Andhavarapu — independent director and audit committee chair of a BSE-listed company. Fifteen years building data platforms and the teams that run them.

The problem I work on

Most organizations can produce a number. Far fewer can show where it came from. The gap between those two things stays invisible until someone asks — a funder, an auditor, a board — and then it becomes a scramble that a few people absorb quietly, every reporting cycle.

That gap is usually not a software problem. Financial data sits in one system, delivery data in another, and the join between them is nobody's job. So it gets done by hand, by someone who is good at it, and the cost never appears in a budget because it is absorbed rather than spent.

I trace numbers from where the work happens through to where it gets reported, and I write down what I find — including when I find nothing worth fixing. I sit on the side of the table that asks how you know, not the side that sells you a system.
